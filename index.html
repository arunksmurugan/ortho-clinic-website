FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions

Does every ACL tear require surgery?

No. Treatment depends on instability, associated injuries, activity demands and individual goals.

When is knee replacement considered?

It may be considered when advanced arthritis causes persistent pain and limitation despite appropriate non-operative care.

What is robotic joint replacement?

Robotic systems assist the surgeon with planning and selected steps of the procedure. The surgeon remains responsible for the operation.

Can I consult before deciding on surgery?

Yes. Consultation can help confirm the diagnosis, review scans and discuss non-operative and surgical options.
