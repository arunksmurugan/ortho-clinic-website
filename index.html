FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Orthopaedic Questions

These answers are general information. Treatment decisions require clinical assessment.

Does every ACL tear require surgery?

No. Treatment depends on instability, associated injuries, age, activity demands and personal goals.

When is knee replacement considered?

It may be considered when advanced arthritis causes persistent pain and functional limitation despite appropriate non-operative treatment.

What is robotic knee replacement?

Robotic systems assist with planning and execution of parts of the procedure. The surgeon remains responsible for the operation.

What should I bring to consultation?

Please bring previous X-rays, MRI or CT scans, reports, prescriptions and a list of current medicines if available.
